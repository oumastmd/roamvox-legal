Roamvox — Support

Roamvox is an offline-first voice translation app: speak, get an instant translation, and download language packs to keep working with no connection.

Need help?

Email md.oumast@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Common questions

Translation isn't working offline

Make sure you've downloaded the language pack for the pair you're using — look for the download icon next to a language in the language picker. Once downloaded, that pair works with no internet connection.

Microphone isn't picking up my voice

Check that Roamvox has microphone and speech recognition permission in your device Settings, and that no other app is currently using the microphone.

How do I delete my history?

Open a session from your history list and use the delete option — history is stored only on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.
